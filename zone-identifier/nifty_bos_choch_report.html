📖 Glossary
BOS — Break of Structure

A BOS occurs when price closes beyond a significant swing high or low in the direction of the existing trend. It confirms that the current trend is still intact and likely to continue.

Bullish BOS: Price closes above a prior swing high in an uptrend — Higher Highs continuation.

CHoCH — Change of Character

A CHoCH occurs when price closes beyond a significant swing high or low against the existing trend. It signals a potential reversal and is the first warning that the market's character may be changing.

Bullish CHoCH: In a downtrend, price closes above last Lower High — potential bottom forming.
Bearish CHoCH: In an uptrend, price closes below last Higher Low — potential top forming.
